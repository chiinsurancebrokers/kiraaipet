---
description: Think through second-order consequences of a decision
argument-hint: <decision or change>
---
For $ARGUMENTS, trace consequences beyond the first effect.
- First-order: what happens directly.
- Second-order: what that causes in users, support, safety, cost, and trust (and who games it).
- Third-order: what it looks like in 6-12 months.
Flag any effect that could make a pet owner delay care or a vet distrust the app. End with: do it / change it / do not do it, and the one thing to measure.
